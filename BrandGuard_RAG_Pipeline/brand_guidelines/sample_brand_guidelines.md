# Sample Brand Guidelines — REPLACE WITH YOUR APPROVED GUIDELINES

## Brand voice
- Use clear, friendly, direct language.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives such as "the best" unless approved evidence is supplied.
- Do not make guarantees about outcomes unless explicitly approved.

## Visual identity
- Use only official logo files supplied by the brand owner.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or redraw the logo.
- Keep sufficient clear space around the logo.
- Use approved brand colors only. Replace this sample with official color names and HEX values.

## Claims and disclaimers
- Every factual product claim must be supported by an approved source.
- Do not invent product specifications, awards, certifications, or customer statistics.
- Include any disclaimer required by the approved campaign brief.

## Review process
- Flag uncertain findings for human review.
- Do not call an advertisement authentic or fraudulent based only on visual appearance.
